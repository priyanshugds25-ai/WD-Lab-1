Tribute to Ratan Tata

Ratan Tata

Ratan Tata is one of India's most respected industrialists and the former chairman of Tata Group. He is known for his visionary leadership and ethical business practices.

Achievements

  • Former Chairman of Tata Group
  • Expanded Tata Group globally
  • Acquired Jaguar and Land Rover
  • Awarded Padma Bhushan
  • Awarded Padma Vibhushan
